## Design Context

### Users
Branch staff in Uganda run the daily desk: find a client, record a payment, disburse a loan, and close the books. Clients use a separate wallet. Investors use a separate sign-in.

### Brand Personality
Calm, precise, and warm. The desk should feel like the wallet: forest green, paper canvas, one obvious next action.

### Aesthetic Direction
Forest is the only primary. Rust is delete, reject, and undo disbursement. Gold stays a warning and data accent, not a button. Staff chrome uses the shared canvas and forest, 8px controls, 12px panels, and fewer borders. The wallet keeps its pill navigation.

### Implementation Defaults
Next.js App Router, React 19, and the existing global CSS. No component library. Buttons are `.primary`, `.secondary-action`, and `BrandActionButton` (`primary` or destructive `gold`).

### Design Principles
- One forest primary per screen. Everything else is quiet.
- Rust means a destructive confirm, and the label says the consequence.
- Daily navigation stays on Clients, Loans, Accounts, and Groups.
- Section titles stay quieter than the figures. Money is tabular and right-aligned.
- Staff and wallet share canvas and forest. They do not share the wallet's pill nav.
